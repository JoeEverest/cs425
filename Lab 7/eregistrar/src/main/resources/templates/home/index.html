Welcome to eRegistrar - the Online Course Registration System

eRegistrar helps students plan and manage their studies each semester. From one place you can browse the course catalogue, register for the classes you need, view your weekly schedule, and drop a course before the deadline.

To get started, use the menu-bar above. Select Browse Courses to see the courses offered this term, together with their credit hours, prerequisites, and available seats. When you are ready, choose Register to add courses to your semester plan.

Key points to remember:
  • You may register for a maximum of 18 credit hours per semester.
  • Make sure you meet every prerequisite before you register for a course.
  • Registration closes at the end of the first week of the term.
  • You can drop a course without a grade penalty within the first two weeks.
eRegistrar keeps a record of the courses you complete, so you can follow your progress toward your degree from one term to the next.
